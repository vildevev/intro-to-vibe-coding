# 🎸 Intro to Vibe Coding

*A course for people who have never written code — and never need to.*

**Vibe coding** is building real software by *describing* what you want to an AI
assistant, reviewing what it does, and steering it — the way a movie director
doesn't operate the camera but still makes the film.

This course teaches you the **survival skills** the AI won't warn you about:
how to never lose your work, how to never leak your passwords, how to fix
things when they break, and how to put what you built on the internet.

You will not memorize commands. Your AI does the typing. **You** learn to make
the decisions — and to check its work.

---

## How this works

- 8 challenges, in order. Each one is 30–60 minutes, mostly *doing*.
- You need one thing installed: an **AI coding assistant** (e.g. ZCode,
  Claude Code, Cursor, or Replit) and a web browser. Challenge 0 covers setup.
- Every challenge follows the same shape:
  **a story → hands-on steps → prompts to copy-paste → a "you passed when" checklist → what to do when it goes wrong.**
- Work in pairs or a group if you can. Comparing disasters is half the fun.

## The 5 Golden Rules of Vibe Coding

1. **Save before you renovate.** Make a git save point before letting the AI
   make big changes. (Challenge 1)
2. **Secrets never leave the drawer.** Passwords and API keys live in `.env`
   and are never pasted into chats, code, or screenshots. (Challenge 2)
3. **The AI is your intern, not your boss.** Brilliant, tireless, and
   occasionally overconfident. You review, you decide. (Challenges 3 & 5)
4. **Errors are clues, not failures.** Red text is treasure map data. (Challenge 4)
5. **One change at a time.** Small requests, reviewed, saved, shipped. (Challenge 6)

## FAQ

**Do I need to learn to code?**
No. You'll learn to *read* enough — like knowing what a street sign means vs.
being a mechanic. You'll pick up vocabulary naturally.

**Which AI assistant should I use?**
Any that can edit files on your computer (ZCode, Claude Code, Cursor) — or a
browser-based one (Replit). The challenges are tool-agnostic; when we say
"ask your AI," any of them works.

**What does this cost?**
The course is free. AI assistants have free tiers good enough for all 8
challenges; paid plans (~$20/month) are faster but not required. Deploying in
Challenge 6 is free (GitHub Pages).

**What if I break something?**
That's the point of Challenge 1. Git is a time machine — and by Challenge 2
you'll know how to verify nothing dangerous got saved.
